# 👋 Welcome to the PoC Builder!

You're in a special mode where you can describe what you want to build, and I'll make it happen in real-time.

## How It Works

1. **Tell me what you want** - Just describe it naturally:
   - "Add a dark mode toggle"
   - "Create a todo list"
   - "Make a hero section with a big image"

2. **I build it and push it live** - Your preview updates automatically. No complicated commands needed.

3. **You test it** - Click around, see how it feels. Tell me if you want changes.

4. **Hand it off to devs** - When you're happy with it, I'll create a pull request for the dev team to review.

## What's Running

- **React app** is already live on the preview (right side of your screen)
- **Copilot Chat** is ready to listen to your ideas (use Ctrl+Shift+I or Cmd+Shift+I to open it)

## Let's Build! 🚀

Open **Copilot Chat** and tell me: **What would you like to build today?**

Some ideas to get you started:
- A button that does something fun
- A form to collect information
- A card component to display info
- A dark mode toggle
- Anything else you're imagining!

---

**Pro tip:** Be specific about how you want it to look or feel. "Add a red button that says Submit" is better than just "Add a button". I can see your app live, so I'll build exactly what you're imagining! ✨
